Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Your privacy is highly important to me. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that may be received or collected when you visit my portfolio website and how it is managed.

1. Information Collection

This website is built as a static personal portfolio showcase. I do not run registration systems, user accounts, or newsletter databases. The only personal data collected is information that you explicitly and voluntarily provide when reaching out to me via email, phone, or connected social networks (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

2. Use of Information

Any communication or contact details shared with me will be used strictly to respond to your specific messages, discuss potential collaborative coding projects, or answer academic and professional inquiries.

3. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not deploy tracking cookies, data analytics pixels, or user-behavior tracking mechanisms. Your casual browsing activity on this site remains entirely anonymous.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

I do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal contact information to any third-party corporations, advertisers, or external organizations. All interactions are handled confidentially.

5. External Third-Party Links

My portfolio contains outward links to independent platforms including GitHub, LinkedIn, and email hosts. If you choose to follow these links, please note that I do not control those external ecosystems. I recommend checking their individual corporate privacy frameworks to understand how they process data.

6. Policy Updates

I may occasionally update this policy to reflect subtle revisions to my website architecture. Any adjustments will be transparently documented on this page with an updated modification date.

7. Contact Me

If you have any questions regarding how your data is handled, feel free to reach out directly via my primary email: vishnuasriram@gmail.com.
